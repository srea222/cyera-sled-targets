# Cyera SLED — Prime Target List

**Version 1.0 · October 2026 · Companion to the Cyera SLED Market Entry Plan (v2)**

This is the working account list for Cyera's State, Local & Education (SLED) build-out: **~105 named organizations across 91 entries**, mapped to the lean three-seller territory model, tiered by when to pursue them, and annotated with the trigger, contract path, and compliance drivers for each.

## The thesis in one line

Governments and universities spent 2026 signing frontier-AI deals faster than they classified the data underneath them — and you cannot scale AI across data you haven't classified. Every signed AI deployment on this list is a pre-qualified Cyera prospect with a funded initiative and a deadline.

## How the list is built

Accounts scored on four factors, equally weighted:

1. **Budget reality** — IT/security spend large enough for a six-figure data-security platform
2. **Procurement speed** — a named contract vehicle available on day one (TX DIR, NASPO PA, state term contract, E&I, TIPS)
3. **Compliance unlock** — GovRAMP mandates (NC, AZ, NV, UT), TX-RAMP, or a deadline-driven framework (CJIS v6.0 Oct 2027, CMS ARC-AMPE, CMMC Phase 2, NSPM-33, IRS 1075, GLBA)
4. **AI trigger** — a signed frontier-model deployment (state or campus), an AI governance mandate, or a breach that made data mapping board-level

## Tier definitions

| Tier | Meaning | Expectation |
|------|---------|-------------|
| 1 | Pursue now — vehicle + trigger both live | First meetings in Q1, pipeline in 1–2 quarters |
| 2 | Engage months 6–18 — trigger live, path slower | Nurture + campaign kits, convert as certifications land |
| 3 | Seed / long-lead — big but slow (NY, NYC, CA state, Midwest) or channel-only (K-12) | Hours per month, not headcount |

## Operating rules this list assumes

- **No pursuit without a named contract path.** Every entry carries its vehicle; if the vehicle isn't live yet (NASPO PA pending, GovRAMP in process), the account is Tier 2/3 by definition.
- **ACV bands are street-price planning numbers** (state $150–400K · county/city $75–200K · university $100–300K · system $250–500K · district $50–125K) with ~25–30% channel economics inside.
- **Tier 3 is seeded, never sold, in Year 1** — NYC PASSPort enrollment, NY OGS recruitment, CA CCSA artifacts, Midwest relationships. Hours per month.
- **Entry points are offices, not people** — names rotate; the CISO/CDO/research-security seats don't.

Built from public sources (October 2026): state and vendor announcements of AI deployments, the DefenseScoop-reported list of 30 universities under DoD research-security audit, NSF HERD FY2024 R&D expenditures, NCES district enrollment, GovRAMP/TX-RAMP program requirements, NASPO/DIR/co-op vehicle documentation, and reported breach disclosures.
